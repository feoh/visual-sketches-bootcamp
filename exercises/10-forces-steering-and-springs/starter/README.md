# Section 10 starter: force modes

Build a force-driven chain or swarm from the pure model in `../shared`. Own the
values in `src/design/force_design.cpp` and replace the starter's seek circles
and ring plus spring triangles, square anchor, and straight links with distinct
geometry. Controls: pointer/arrows move the mode's target or anchor; 1 selects
seek; 2 selects spring chain;
Space switches mode; P pauses; R resets; M enters a still reduced-motion view.

Run `CXX=g++ ../../../tests/run-section-10-tests.sh`, then generate/build with
`../../../scripts/section-10.sh`. The automated gate checks state and math, not
pixels, launch, accessibility, or originality.
